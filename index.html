Our Story & Passion

Nestled in the heart of Bypass Gaya, Desi Street Restaurant & Café was born from a simple dream — to bring the vibrant, unapologetic flavours of Indian street food under one warm roof. Since 2018 we have been serving Gaya's most beloved dishes, made fresh every single day.
